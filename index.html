How to read this map

AI embedding

Measures how strongly the satellite-observed landscape signal changed. It does not identify the cause by itself.

DEA Land Cover

Provides broad and detailed annual land-cover classes and the proportion of each region represented by the dominant class.

NDVI

Measures vegetation greenness and indicates net greening, browning or no clear net change.

Complete regional history

All 13,784 regions have annual records from 2017 to 2024. One small region has an unknown detailed DEA class in 2021.

Annual satellite imagery

Esri World Imagery Wayback provides a high-detail archived basemap release for each selected year. An archive can contain imagery captured earlier, so the map reports the actual local capture date and resolution where available.
